# Brag Plan: Meme Capsule

## What is this app?
Meme Capsule is a tiny curated meme PWA where one tap spawns one approved laugh, with a daily drop, sharing, reactions, and an admin report inbox behind it.

## The angle
Treat the app like a beautifully over-designed capsule machine for a problem nobody needed solved: getting one curated meme without opening an endless feed. The joke is the contrast between the absurdly focused consumer flow and the very serious moderation dashboard keeping the chaos approved.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Warm paper background. The line “One tap. One curated laugh.” lands like a product launch claim while a single yellow capsule button pulses underneath.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The actual working-app flow: “Spawn a Random Meme” becomes “Shaking the capsule...” and reveals a bright curated meme card with a “Rare” badge.
- The real action row appears: Share, Save, Like, and LOL, with the like count and local LOL reaction popping in sequence.
- A sharp cut to the real reports concept: “Someone reported the meme.” A compact reports card shows Pending, Resolve, and Dismiss controls.

## Outro / punchline
“Meme Capsule” resolves to “One tap. One curated laugh.” Then the small final line: “Curated chaos, responsibly shipped.”

## User flow worth showing
Entry: land on the empty capsule screen. Key action: tap “Spawn a Random Meme.” Result: a rarity-tagged meme appears, followed by sharing/reaction controls and a report review card.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: warm, playful product launch for an absurdly focused meme machine
- Interpretation: comfortable rhythm, friendly mixed-case typography, clean wipes, and humor from treating one meme as a serious product category.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#fff8e7` with radial yellow/mint gradients
- Accent: `#ffcc4d`
- Text: `#16120f`
- Display font: Inter/system sans, heavy headline weight
- Body font: Inter/system sans
- Strongest visual element: the rounded capsule stage, yellow rarity pills, and large “One tap. One curated laugh.” headline.

## Share copy (draft)
Made Meme Capsule: one tap, one curated laugh, and just enough reporting infrastructure to keep the chaos approved.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm upbeat bed with restrained UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: start under the hook at 0.28 volume, lift slightly for the meme reveal, fade under the final line.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `composition/assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; strong cues at 1.60s, 3.70s, 5.80s, 8.96s, 12.65s, and 17.91s. Major reveals target 3.70s, 8.96s, and 17.91s. Sequential action chips use the 10.54-12.12s beat window but hold the full row for readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the capsule glow and report-card presence breathe with the music bed if available. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate and motion-matched; capsule tap, meme reveal, action-chip arrivals, and final logo hit only.
- Audio-coupled moments: simulated tap on the spawn button, card reveal, sequential Share/Save/Like/LOL chips, report status landing.
- Restraint rule: no sound on every word; the joke should remain readable without audio.

Sequential/interaction: yes — one visible tap, then one card reveal.

### Scene 3 — One meme, four ways to react — 4.6s

Sequential/interaction: yes — four action chips in sequence, then hold.
Audio intent: three light UI ticks and one warmer confirmation accent.
Audio-coupled idea: chip entrances can align to every other beat in the 10.54-12.12s window.
Music: steady and playful.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Approved chaos — 3.4s

Audio intent: one dry notification accent; keep the bed restrained.
Audio-coupled idea: status badge lands near the 17.91s strong cue.
Music: lightly ducked, deadpan-comic.

### Scene 5 — Responsible punchline — 3.4s

Music: fade out under final line.
